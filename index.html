Karnataka dropped the old slab system: since FY 2024–25 every domestic unit costs the same flat rate, whether it is your 10th unit or your 400th. What pushes bills up now is the fixed charge on your sanctioned load, FAC, and one-off lines like the true-up.

Bill higher than expected? Check these first

The usual culprits behind a Bengaluru bill that looks wrong.

  • The FY25 true-up lineFrom May 2026, bills carry a separate “FY25 True up Charges” item at 56 paise per unit, recovering BESCOM’s 2024–25 revenue gap of ₹2,068 crore. It runs until April 2027. On 250 units that is ₹140 most people never budgeted for.
  • You crossed 200 units on Gruha JyothiOne unit over 200 and the subsidy disappears for that month: the entire bill becomes payable, not just the extra unit.
  • A long billing cycleMeter readings are not always 30 days apart. A 35-day cycle bills 35 days of usage at once, which looks like a spike but is the same daily rate.
  • Fixed charges on sanctioned loadYou pay ₹150 per kW every month even at zero usage. A 5 kW connection starts at ₹750 before a single unit is consumed.
  • Arrears or an average-billing correctionIf an earlier month was billed on average (door locked, meter unread), the catch-up lands in a later bill as arrears or an adjustment.

How Gruha Jyothi changes the maths

From BESCOM’s scheme FAQ. Turn it on in the calculator to see it applied to your numbers.

Your entitlement is personal. It is your average monthly consumption in FY 2022–23 plus 10%, capped at 200 units. It is printed on your bill. A household that averaged 150 units gets 165 free units, not 200.

Within entitlement: zero bill, the government settles it with BESCOM. Above entitlement but at or under 200 units: you pay for the extra units only. Above 200 units: the full bill for that month is payable and the subsidy is lost for the month.

Tenants, note: the entitlement belongs to the meter (RR number), not to you. If you moved in recently, the free-unit limit reflects the previous occupant’s usage, which is why two identical flats on the same street can get very different bills.
